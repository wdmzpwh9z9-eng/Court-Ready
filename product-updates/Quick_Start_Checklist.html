Set Up Your System

Twenty minutes today, then you are running

Do these seven things once. You will not have to think about setup again.

  1. Unzip your downloadYou should see a START HERE folder — Calendar, Case Record Form, Master Index, Manual, AI Prompts — and four numbered folders: 01_Case_Management_File, 02_Reference_Library, 03_Exhibit_Production, 04_Backups. That is your entire system.
  2. Choose your template formatHTML opens in any browser. PDF is a fillable form. Both do the same thing. Pick one and stick with it.
  3. Open the CalendarDouble-click Calendar.html. Click Custody Schedule Setup. Toggle each day between Mine and Theirs, enter your exchange time and location. Save.
  4. Open the Master IndexDouble-click Court_Ready_Master_Index.xlsx. Go to Lists & Setup and review the factor tags and dropdown options. Your Dashboard is now live.
  5. Set up your Reference LibraryIn 02_Reference_Library, place existing court orders in Court_Orders and your parenting plan in Parenting_Plan. Medical records, school records, attorney documents and contacts go in their named folders.
  6. Read Part 1 of the ManualTen minutes. It teaches the whole workflow, and every step after this assumes it.
  7. Document your first eventSomething will happen. Open the Case Record Form. Check the boxes. Write the facts in the three blocks. Save it as a PDF into its event folder. Paste the generated row into the Master Index. Done.
You're ready
Ten minutes a day. One event at a time.
The system does the rest.

Evidence Collection

Check what you have, spot what is missing

Print this page and check off each type as you collect it. Not every case needs every type — but reviewing the list is how you catch the gap before someone else does.

Communications
  • Text message threads — full, unedited, with headers
  • Email exchanges — complete threads as PDF
  • Co-parenting app messages, exported
  • Voicemail recordings or transcripts
  • Social media posts or messages — full screenshots
  • Letters or written correspondence
Medical & Health
  • Doctor visit summaries
  • Prescription records and pharmacy receipts
  • Dental records and visit receipts
  • Therapy or counseling records
  • Insurance claims and EOBs
  • Immunization records
  • ER or urgent care discharge papers
School & Education
  • Report cards and progress reports
  • Attendance records — official printout
  • Teacher or counselor communications
  • IEP or 504 plan documents
  • School event attendance records
  • Homework and project involvement
Financial
  • Receipts for child-related expenses
  • Childcare invoices and payments
  • Activity and enrollment fees
  • Clothing and school supply purchases
  • Medical copays and prescription costs
  • Travel and transportation expenses
Schedule & Exchanges
  • Exchange time photos or screenshots
  • Late arrival documentation
  • Schedule deviation records
  • Makeup day agreements
  • Holiday and vacation schedule records
Legal & Court
  • Current court orders
  • Parenting plan
  • Police reports
  • CPS reports or records
  • Attorney correspondence
Do not

Put attorney correspondence in a binder or send it with your exhibits. It is privileged. It stays in 02_Reference_Library/Attorney_Documents and goes nowhere else.

No Scanner? No Problem.

Your phone is your scanner — three free options

Your smartphone creates clean, professional PDF scans of any document — receipts, school forms, court papers, handwritten notes. Pick one method and use it every time.

Option 1

iPhone Notes App

Built in. Nothing to download.
  1. Open Notes and create a new note.
  2. Tap the camera icon at the bottom.
  3. Select "Scan Documents."
  4. Position the document — it auto-captures when aligned.
  5. Tap Save when done.
  6. Tap the share icon, then Save as PDF to Files.
  7. Rename your copy to the court-ready name the form generates, then file it in the event folder.
Option 2

Google Drive App

iPhone and Android. Saves to cloud.
  1. Open Google Drive and tap the + button.
  2. Select "Scan."
  3. Position and capture the document.
  4. Add additional pages if needed.
  5. Tap Save — it becomes a PDF automatically.
  6. Rename your copy to the court-ready name the form generates, then file it in the event folder.
Option 3

Microsoft Lens

Free. Best for multi-page documents.
  1. Download Microsoft Lens from the App Store or Google Play.
  2. Open the app and select "Document" mode.
  3. Capture each page.
  4. The app auto-crops and enhances for readability.
  5. Export as PDF.
  6. Rename your copy to the court-ready name the form generates, then file it in the event folder.
EVT-ID_YYYY-MM-DD_EventType_ExLetter_EvidenceType

The folder is the first three parts; each evidence file adds its exhibit letter and type. The form builds both — you never type them.

Tip

Scan immediately. Do not photograph evidence and leave it in your camera roll. Scan it, name it, file it, log it. One touch, done.
