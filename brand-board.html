Typography

Three faces, three jobs, no substitutes
Display · Outfit Attorney-ready records. Weights 500–800. Titles, section heads, pull-quotes. Tight tracking (−.015em).
Body · Figtree Three factual blocks, two minutes per entry. The system does the rest — including a dashboard that shows exactly where the record is strong. Weights 400–700. All running text. Line-height ≈ 1.6, measure ≤ 65 characters.
Utility · IBM Plex Mono EVT-0012_2026-07-18_Exchange File names, timestamps, exhibit codes, footers, eyebrow labels. The "evidence texture" that makes the brand unmistakable.

Do / Don't

The rules that keep it expensive
Always
  • Lead with Pine; let Clay do the work
  • One Marigold highlight per section
  • Record Mark on every cover, avatar, favicon
  • Mono labels for anything that is data
  • Whitespace as a feature — breathing room sells calm
Never
  • New hues — no purples, bright blues, pastels
  • Dark backgrounds behind body text
  • Stock gavels, courtrooms, or crying children
  • Hype copy, ALL-CAPS urgency, emoji on product surfaces
  • A fourth typeface
