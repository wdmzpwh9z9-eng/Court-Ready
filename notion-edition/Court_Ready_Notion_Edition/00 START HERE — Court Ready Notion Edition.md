# Court Ready™ — Notion Edition

**Vejovis Intelligence Co.™ · Custody Documentation System**

Welcome. This is the same Court Ready™ system as the download package, rebuilt as a Notion workspace: the Case Record Form as a duplicatable page template, and your Event Index, Exhibit Index, Expense Summary, and Visitation Log as Notion databases.

> **One principle drives everything: document what a camera would see.**
> Times, dates, direct quotes, actions. Not feelings. Not accusations.

---

## How to import this into Notion (5 minutes)

1. Open Notion on a computer (import works best on desktop, not the phone app).
2. In the left sidebar, click **Settings** → **Settings** → **Import**.
3. Choose **Text & Markdown**.
4. Select **all the files in this folder** (or the whole unzipped folder).
5. Notion creates a page for each document and a **database** for each spreadsheet file. Drag them under one parent page named **Court Ready™** to keep everything together.

That's it. The sample rows in each database show you exactly how entries should look — replace them with your own when you're ready.

---

## Your daily workflow in Notion

1. Something happens → open **01 Case Record Form Template** → duplicate it (⌘/Ctrl-D or ••• → Duplicate).
2. Rename the duplicate with the event ID and date, e.g. `EVT-0003 2026-07-21 Exchange`.
3. Fill in the checkboxes and the three factual blocks. Two minutes for a routine event.
4. Add one row to the **02 Event Index** database.
5. Attach evidence (photos, screenshots) directly on the event page — Notion stores them with the entry.
6. Once a week: ten-minute review. Fill gaps, complete follow-ups, back up.

---

## The rules that keep your record court-ready

- Use **Parent A** (you) and **Parent B** (the other parent). Neutral labels keep the record readable by anyone.
- Facts only: times, places, names, direct quotes. Feelings tell you *what* to document — they do not go in the entry.
- Never crop, edit, or rename original evidence files. Work on copies.
- Notify Parent B where appropriate and record when and how. A pattern of you informing and them not responding builds itself over time.
