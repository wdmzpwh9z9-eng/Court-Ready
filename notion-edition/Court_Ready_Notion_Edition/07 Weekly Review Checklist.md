# 07 Weekly Review Checklist

**Ten minutes, once a week, same day every week. This is what keeps the record complete.**

---

## The review

- [ ] Open the **02 Event Index** database — scan the week's rows
- [ ] Any event that happened but wasn't recorded? Duplicate **01 Case Record Form Template** and capture it now, while memory is fresh
- [ ] Any quick captures or phone notes from the week? Transfer them to full entries
- [ ] Any **follow-up actions** due? Complete or reschedule them
- [ ] Any new **evidence** (screenshots, photos, documents)? Attach to the right event page and add a row to **03 Exhibit Index**
- [ ] Any **expenses** this week? Add rows to **04 Expense Summary** with reimbursement status
- [ ] Update **05 Visitation Log** — mark the week's scheduled events as kept or deviated
- [ ] Back up: export or duplicate your Court Ready™ workspace (Notion: Settings → Export)

## Once a month, add:

- [ ] Run the **Monthly Review AI prompt** (page 06) on your recent entries
- [ ] Check factor coverage — which best-interests areas are thin? Plan what to document next
- [ ] Verify evidence originals are safely backed up outside Notion too

---

> **Why this matters:** a record built ten minutes at a time, every week, is the record that holds up. The night-before-a-hearing scramble is exactly what this system exists to prevent.
